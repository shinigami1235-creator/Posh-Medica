Company Milestone

  1. 2011

    The Beginning

    Founded Wiwan Aesthetic Clinic by expert dermatologists.

  2. 2012

    Posh Medica Supplement Trading Company

    Established Posh Medica, focusing on fiber-based dietary supplements, sold in 300+ stores nationwide.

  3. 2016

    Posh Lab R&D laboratory

    Launched Posh Lab for R&D and pilot production.

  4. 2018

    Grand Opening of Posh Medica

    Officially launched as a full-service manufacturer and distributor for in-house and OEM brands.

  5. 2023

    Relocated the Manufacturing Facility

    Expanded production to Lam Luk Ka, Pathum Thani, increasing capacity 20-fold.

  6. 2025

    Posh Medica Lifetech

    Becomes a public company, marking a step toward sustainable global growth.

Message from Company

To Our Shareholders and Stakeholders,

In the year 2026, the global landscape of politics and economics among major world powers continues to evolve, inevitably impacting Thailand’s economy. Forecasts indicate that Thailand’s economic growth will remain positive, although at a moderate level. Nevertheless, supportive factors remain, including the nationwide parliamentary elections, which will provide clearer direction for national policies and governance. In addition, the tourism sector continues to show steady progress.

Recognizing these circumstances, the Company and its management team have proactively prepared across all key areas to ensure the achievement of our strategic objectives. We remain committed to driving the Company forward and accomplishing the goals we have set for the future.

As a fully integrated manufacturer and distributor of dietary supplements and cosmetics, operating under internationally certified standards, we extend our sincere appreciation to our valued customers, partners, and business allies for their continued trust and support.

The Company is committed to developing high-quality, safe, and consumer-oriented products by integrating modern innovations and technologies throughout every stage of our production processes. We conduct our business with a deep sense of responsibility towards society and the environment, guided by our philosophy: “Toward Medical Excellence.”

Over the past year, we have expanded our distribution channels nationwide, both offline and online, to enhance accessibility for consumers. We believe that continuous adaptation and improvement are key to sustainable growth in an ever-changing economic landscape. In addition, we are actively building new strategic partnerships to strengthen our logistics and distribution systems, enabling us to respond to customer needs with greater speed and efficiency.

The Company also prioritizes building strong relationships with local communities by supporting small businesses and promoting local employment through the ongoing expansion of our manufacturing capacity. Furthermore, we are committed to developing innovative new products that align with global health and beauty trends, ensuring we continue to meet the evolving demands of modern consumers. Our strategic plan includes expanding into international markets to seize new growth opportunities and enhance the strength of our brand.

On behalf of the Board of Directors, management, and all employees of the Company, I would like to express our heartfelt gratitude to our customers, partners, shareholders, and stakeholders for your continued confidence and partnership. We remain steadfast in our mission to pursue sustainable growth and to deliver the very best products to consumers across Thailand and beyond.

Dr. Mangkorn DhanasarnsilpChairman of the BoardPosh Medica Lifetech Public Company Limited
