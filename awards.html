Accreditation & Recognition

At Posh Medica Lifetech Public Company Limited, we proudly affirm the quality of our products through the prestigious accolades we have received. In 2014, the company was honored with the Quality Businessperson Award from the Foundation of Science and Technology Council of Thailand (FSTT). The award was presented by H.E. Mr. Plodprasop Suraswadi, Privy Councillor, in recognition of individuals who have achieved success in life and demonstrated dedication to activities benefiting society.

In 2016, the company received the Outstanding Product of the Year 2016 Award from the Council for the Promotion and Development of Thai Business and Economy (CPDE). This accolade affirms that our products meet high quality standards and are in full compliance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
